01 / Executive summary

Do sensors and community voices make the library's advice better?

The hypothesis

Putting station readings and what residents said at workshops and festivals beside the published sources will give landowners answers that are more specific to their place, easier to trace to a source, and end in a clear next step.

The test: ask the same three questions of a web search, of Chat with Land as it runs today, and of Chat with Land after the milestone 3 material and station readings are added.

What we measure: local specificity, whether each claim can be traced to a source, and whether the answer ends in something the landowner can do next.

Answers on this page are copied from recorded sessions. Where a session has not happened yet, the column says so.

02 / Fire preparation

How should I prepare my land for fire this year?(when, how, and with what)

Web searchYou assemble the answer

When: the legal clearing deadline comes from national news and the municipality's notices, each found separately.

How: ICNF guidance on fuel-management strips is written for the whole country, not this slope.

With what: contractors, grazing and firebreak methods sit in unrelated results.

Still missing: anything about this basin, or this parcel.
